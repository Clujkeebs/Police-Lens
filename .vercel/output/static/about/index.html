How PolicyLens Works

Turning complex legal documents into clear, actionable insights

Our Mission

Privacy policies and terms of service are often written to be intentionally difficult to understand. Studies show that the average American would need to spend 76 full work days reading all the privacy policies they encounter in a year.

PolicyLens was created to bridge this gap. We believe you shouldn't need a law degree to understand your digital rights.

How Analysis Works

PolicyLens uses a sophisticated rule-based analysis engine that examines privacy policies and terms of service documents to identify key patterns and potential concerns.

1. Text Extraction

Whether you paste text directly or provide a URL, we extract the policy content and prepare it for analysis.

2. Pattern Detection

Our engine scans for 100+ patterns across 12 key categories including data collection, sharing practices, user rights, and security measures.

3. Red Flag Detection

We flag concerning clauses like unlimited liability, class action waivers, data selling provisions, and other potentially problematic terms.

4. Risk Scoring

Based on all detected patterns, we calculate a comprehensive risk score from 0-100 to help you quickly assess the policy's privacy implications.

What We Look For

Red Flags

  • Data selling provisions
  • Unlimited liability clauses
  • Class action waivers
  • Keystroke or screen recording
  • Continuous location tracking
  • Vague data sharing language
  • Automatic data retention
  • Lack of deletion rights

Good Signs

  • End-to-end encryption
  • GDPR/CCPA compliance statements
  • Clear data deletion processes
  • Minimal data collection
  • Regular security audits
  • Bug bounty programs
  • No third-party ad networks
  • Open source components

Privacy & Security

Your privacy matters to us. Here's what you should know:

  • No Account Required: We don't require sign-ups or logins. Your analysis history is stored locally in your browser.
  • No Data Storage: We don't store your pasted policies or analysis results on our servers.
  • URL Fetching: When you provide a URL, we fetch it once to extract the policy content. We don't maintain copies.
  • No Tracking: We don't use tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or analytics that could compromise your privacy.
