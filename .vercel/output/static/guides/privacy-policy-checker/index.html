What the Analysis Includes

Required Sections

Checks for all mandatory disclosures including data collected, purposes, legal basis, retention periods, and third-party sharing.

User Rights

Verifies inclusion of data subject rights: access, rectification, erasure, portability, and objection.

Contact Information

Confirms presence of valid contact details for privacy inquiries and data protection officer information.

Cookie & Tracking

Reviews cookie disclosures, tracking technologies used, and opt-out mechanisms provided.

Children's Privacy

Checks for COPPA compliance and age-gating provisions for services directed to children.

International Transfers

Validates cross-border data transfer mechanisms and adequacy provisions for international users.

Important

Privacy regulations carry significant penalties. GDPR fines can reach €20 million or 4% of annual global revenue. CCPA violations cost $2,500 per inadvertent violation and $7,500 for intentional violations.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the analysis take?

The analysis completes in under 30 seconds for most privacy policies. Large policies with extensive provisions may take slightly longer but rarely exceed one minute.

Does the tool store my privacy policy?

No, we do not store your privacy policy. All analysis is performed in real-time and the document is discarded immediately after analysis completes. Your data remains completely confidential.

What regulations does the checker support?

Currently supports GDPR (EU), UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA (California), and CDPA (Virginia). Additional regulations including PIPEDA (Canada) and LGPD (Brazil) are coming soon.

Can I check multiple policies?

Yes, you can analyze unlimited policies. For businesses managing multiple properties, we offer bulk analysis capabilities in our premium plan.

Is the analysis legally binding?

The tool provides automated analysis and recommendations based on regulatory requirements. It does not constitute legal advice. For critical compliance needs, consult with a qualified privacy attorney.

Pre-Check Checklist

Before analyzing your policy, ensure you have the following information ready:

  • Current privacy policy document or URL
  • List of all data collected from users
  • Third-party services and vendors you share data with
  • Cookie and tracking technologies used
  • Countries where you operate or target users
  • Target audience age ranges
